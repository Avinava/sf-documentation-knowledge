# Undertand Invoices and Payouts

From the Invoices and Payouts tabs, you can see all of the payment documentation generated by Stripe and get a full picture of the related deadlines. To understand how payouts and invoices are created and tracked, let’s look at a larger view of the lifecycle.

The diagram shows how Salesforce moves you and your customer through the process. After an offer is accepted and an order is generated, licenses are automatically provisioned and your customer can start using your products. From there, Stripe generates an invoice, sends it to your customer, and the invoice status is set to Pending. You can see the invoice and its status from the Invoices tab.

![Overview of the Partner Marketplace order to payout lifecycle.](/docs/resources/img/en-us/260.0?doc_id=appexchange%2Fimages%2Fpartner_marketplace_payouts.png&folder=packagingGuide)

Your customer receives the invoice via email and navigates to Stripe to pay their balance. When the balance is settled, the invoice status moves to Paid. The customer payment is divided into the various buckets, including applicable taxes, the Salesforce revenue share, and partner net proceeds.

-   **[Partner Payouts Overview](atlas.en-us.packagingGuide.meta/packagingGuide/partner_marketplace_payouts.htm#partner_marketplace_payouts)**
    Payouts refer to the net revenue that partners receive from settled customer payments. Stripe, on behalf of Salesforce, consolidates settled revenue for all customer orders into a single, daily payout. That means Stripe rounds up all your customer payments and deposits the net revenue into your account. If on a given day, you haven’t received payments or a submitted payment hasn’t settled yet, you won’t receive a payout.
-   **[Calculate Your Expected Payout](atlas.en-us.packagingGuide.meta/packagingGuide/partner_marketplace_calculate_payout.htm)**
    To calculate your expected payout, look at the relationships between your order items, your products, and the related product catalog.
