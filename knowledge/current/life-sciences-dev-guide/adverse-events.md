# Adverse Events

> Adverse Events data model helps you in managing and documenting any unfavorable or
    unintended signs, symptoms, or diseases that occur in participants during the research study.
    These adverse events can include a wide range of physical or psychological effects that varies
    from mild discomfort to serious health complications.

# Adverse Events

Adverse Events data model helps you in managing and documenting any unfavorable or unintended signs, symptoms, or diseases that occur in participants during the research study. These adverse events can include a wide range of physical or psychological effects that varies from mild discomfort to serious health complications.
